# 06 — Sentence Reformation

Targets long, tangled, broken sentences. Trains the brain to think
in short, punchy units: **Idea → Why/How → So what.**

## How to use

1. Load `00-system-setup.md` first.
2. Send a message like: `SENTENCE REFORM`
3. Paste this prompt with your sentence below it.

## Prompt

```
I will paste a long, broken, or tangled sentence I produced. For
each one, do this:

1. Diagnose why it failed:
   - too many ideas?
   - wrong order?
   - missing subject or verb?
   - passive sprawl?
   - subordinate clause overload?
2. Break it into 2–3 short sentences. Each: ONE idea, 12–18 words.
3. Show the original vs. reformatted side by side.
4. Teach me the pattern: [Idea] → [Why/How] → [So what].
   Apply it to 3 of my sentences.

Then drill me live:
- Give me 5 complex ideas. I will turn each into 3 short sentences
  in under 30 seconds. Time me. Grade clarity and speed.

Goal: train my brain to think in short, punchy units instead of
long tangled ones. No praise. Just the rep.

Here is my sentence:
[PASTE HERE]
```

## Live-drill ideas to feed the coach

When the coach asks for 5 complex ideas, use these:

1. The system kept failing in production.
2. My manager disagreed with the design direction.
3. I had to ship the feature in two weeks.
4. The customer threatened to leave for a competitor.
5. The codebase was undocumented and three engineers had left.

## Target: 12–18 words per sentence

If a sentence is over 25 words, it almost always needs to be cut.
The pattern is simple:

1. State the idea.
2. Explain why or how.
3. State the consequence or takeaway.

Three short sentences beat one long one. Always.
