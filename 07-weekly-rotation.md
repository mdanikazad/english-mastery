# 07 — Weekly Rotation

One mode per day. The point is to rotate so no single weakness hides.
Sundays are a full simulation. Treat it like a real interview.

| Day | Mode | Primary target |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | `01-fluency-drill.md` | Kill fillers + slow the pace |
| Tue | `03-interview-answer.md` | STAR + specifics (no vague) |
| Wed | `04-storytelling.md` | Hook + detail |
| Thu | `05-mock-interview.md` (realistic) | Pressure + recovery |
| Fri | `02-pronunciation.md` | 3 weak sounds + connected speech |
| Sat | `06-sentence-reform.md` | Short, punchy units |
| Sun | `05-mock-interview.md` (brutal) | Full 60-min simulation |

## Daily session shape (45 min)

1. Warm up (5 min) — speak for 2 minutes on any topic. Just flow.
2. Main drill (30 min) — load the day's mode, do 3–5 reps. Reply
   `next` between reps so the coach never repeats a topic.
3. Cool down (10 min) — re-tell your best answer of the day in 30
   seconds.

## Monthly checkpoints

End of every month, re-record yourself answering:

- Tell me about yourself.
- What is your biggest weakness?
- Describe a project you led.

Compare month-over-month. If the answer is still vague, the
`03-interview-answer.md` coach will tell you. Trust the rubric.

## The "real world" reps

AI builds the engine. You ship the engine in low-stakes rooms first:

- Standup updates without notes.
- Voice notes to friends instead of texts.
- One question asked in a meeting each day.
- One story told in 1:1 conversations each week.

Skill without reps is theater. Reps without skill is noise. You
need both, in that order.
