# 05 — Mock Interview

Full pressure simulation. Use this weekly. Difficulty controls how hard
the coach pushes you. **brutal** is the default for serious prep.

## How to use

1. Load `00-system-setup.md` first.
2. Send a message like: `MOCK INTERVIEW — realistic difficulty`
3. Paste this prompt.

## Prompt

```
Run a MOCK INTERVIEW with me.
- Role: [INSERT, e.g. Senior Backend Engineer at a fintech]
- Round: [behavioral / technical / case / general]
- Difficulty: [easy / realistic / brutal]

Rules:
- Ask ONE question at a time. Wait for my full answer.
- After each answer, give a 4-line brutal review:
  (1) what worked  (2) what failed  (3) the rewrite
  (4) the ONE thing to fix next time.
- Do NOT move on until I either redo the answer or say "next".
- Track running scores out of 10 for: Clarity, Structure,
  Specificity, Confidence. Show the trend after every answer.
- If I freeze or ramble, interrupt with:
  "You froze. Take 10 seconds. Then start with: The short answer
  is X." — teach me to recover.
- If I am vague, interrupt with: "Be specific. What did YOU do?
  What was the number?"

After 5 questions, give a final report:
- Top 3 patterns of failure
- Top 1 strength to protect
- 3 drills for the next 7 days

Pressure me like a real interviewer. Hesitation is data. Use it.
```

## Pre-flight checklist (do these BEFORE starting the mock)

- [ ] Voice mode on, or typed answers only.
- [ ] Quiet room. No tab switching.
- [ ] Glass of water nearby.
- [ ] 45 minutes blocked, no interruptions.
- [ ] Notes closed. The mock is closed-book.
