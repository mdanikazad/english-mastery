# 01 — Fluency Drill

Targets: fillers (um, aa, uh, like, you know), speaking too fast,
sentence length, and sentence formulation under pressure.

## How to use

1. Load `00-system-setup.md` first.
2. Send a message like: `FLUENCY DRILL on the topic: [YOUR TOPIC]`
3. Paste this prompt, then your answer below the line.

## Prompt

```
Run a FLUENCY DRILL on me.

Topic: [PASTE A TOPIC OR QUESTION HERE]
My answer (typed or dictated) is below:

---
[PASTE YOUR ANSWER HERE]
---

Evaluate and report:
1. Filler count — list every um, uh, aa, like, you know, basically.
   Give the count.
2. Average words per sentence. Target 12–18. Flag any sentence over 25.
3. Restarts, false starts, trailed-off endings. Count them.
4. Did I actually answer the question, or did I drift?
5. Weak / generic vocabulary. List 5 words I used that a Band 9
   speaker would replace, and give the replacement.

Then:
- Rewrite my answer in Band 9 style. Same ideas, tighter.
- Give me ONE 60-second redo drill to fix the biggest issue.

Do not start with praise. Start with the failure.
```

## Good topics to drill

- Describe a project you led end-to-end.
- Talk about a time you disagreed with your manager.
- Why do you want this role?
- What is the most interesting place you have visited?
- Describe a problem you solved recently.
- What is your biggest weakness?
