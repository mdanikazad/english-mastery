# 03 — Interview Answer

Targets the "vague" feedback you keep getting. Vague is the disease.
Specifics are the cure. This prompt forces both.

## How to use

1. Load `00-system-setup.md` first.
2. Send a message like: `INTERVIEW ANSWER`
3. Paste this prompt with your question, answer, and target role filled in.

## Prompt

```
Coach me on interview answers. I will give you:
- Question: [PASTE HERE]
- My current answer (typed or spoken): [PASTE HERE]
- Role I am applying for: [INSERT, e.g. Senior Backend Engineer]

Evaluate my answer on this checklist. Be ruthless.

1. Did I answer the actual question, or dance around it?
2. Did I use a clear structure? (STAR: Situation, Task, Action,
   Result — or PAR: Problem, Action, Result)
3. Did I include specifics? Numbers, names, timeframes, concrete
   details. Count them. If zero, say "ZERO SPECIFICS".
4. Did I show MY role, or did I hide behind "we"?
5. Did I land a measurable result?
6. Was the opening strong, or generic ("I am a hardworking
   person...")? If generic, mark it WEAK OPENING.

Then:
- Score clarity 1–10.
- If vague, write VAGUE and name what is missing in one line.
- Give me a model answer (top 1% candidate) using the same facts.
- Ask me 2 pressure follow-ups a senior interviewer would ask, so
  I can practice defending my answer.

Vague is the disease. Specifics are the cure. Pressure me.
```

## STAR / PAR cheat sheet

- **S**ituation: 1 sentence of context. Who, where, when.
- **T**ask: what was the problem or the goal?
- **A**ction: what did **you** do? Be specific. No "we" without
  explaining your part.
- **R**esult: a number, a percentage, a before/after.

If you cannot fill the **A** with specifics, you are hiding. Rewrite.

## Common vague openings to avoid

- "I am a hardworking person."
- "I am a team player."
- "I am passionate about technology."
- "I have always wanted to work at your company."

Replace each with a fact, a number, or a story.
