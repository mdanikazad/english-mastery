# 03 — Interview Answer

Targets the "vague" feedback you keep getting. Vague is the disease.
Specifics are the cure. This prompt forces both.

## How to use

1. Load `00-system-setup.md` first.
2. Send: `INTERVIEW ANSWER` (no question — the coach picks).
3. The coach asks a question, you answer, the coach critiques.
4. Reply `next` for a fresh, non-repeated question.

## Prompt

```
Coach me on interview answers. YOU PICK THE QUESTION. DO NOT ASK
ME FOR A QUESTION.

Maintain an internal "asked questions" list for this chat. Never
repeat a question you have already asked me.

QUESTION POOL — rotate randomly between these categories:
A) Behavioral / STAR (workplace, project, conflict, failure,
   leadership, disagreement, tight deadline, going above and
   beyond, handling ambiguity, mentoring, prioritization under
   pressure).
B) Situational / hypothetical (what would you do if..., how would
   you handle a customer who..., tell me about a time you had to
   learn something fast).
C) "Tell me about yourself" variants (60-second, 90-second,
   technical-emphasis, leadership-emphasis).
D) Common weak-openers trap questions: "What is your biggest
   weakness?", "Why should we hire you?", "Why are you leaving
   your current role?", "Where do you see yourself in 5 years?",
   "Tell me about a time you failed."
E) Daily-life micro-pitch (explain a technical concept to a
   non-technical friend; describe a recent decision you made;
   describe a process you own end-to-end).

Bias 60% toward A and D (the real failure zones), 20% B, 10% C,
10% E.

EVALUATION CHECKLIST (be ruthless):
1. Did I answer the actual question, or dance around it?
2. Did I use a clear structure? (STAR: Situation, Task, Action,
   Result — or PAR: Problem, Action, Result)
3. Did I include specifics? Numbers, names, timeframes, concrete
   details. Count them. If zero, say "ZERO SPECIFICS".
4. Did I show MY role, or did I hide behind "we"?
5. Did I land a measurable result?
6. Was the opening strong, or generic ("I am a hardworking
   person...")? If generic, mark it WEAK OPENING.

Then:
- Score clarity 1–10.
- If vague, write VAGUE and name what is missing in one line.
- Give me a model answer (top 1% candidate) using the same facts.
- Ask me 2 pressure follow-ups a senior interviewer would ask, so
  I can practice defending my answer.
- End by asking me to reply `next` for a fresh question. Do not
  start the next question yourself.

Vague is the disease. Specifics are the cure. Pressure me.
```

## STAR / PAR cheat sheet

- **S**ituation: 1 sentence of context. Who, where, when.
- **T**ask: what was the problem or the goal?
- **A**ction: what did **you** do? Be specific. No "we" without
  explaining your part.
- **R**esult: a number, a percentage, a before/after.

If you cannot fill the **A** with specifics, you are hiding. Rewrite.

## Common vague openings to avoid

- "I am a hardworking person."
- "I am a team player."
- "I am passionate about technology."
- "I have always wanted to work at your company."

Replace each with a fact, a number, or a story.
